Explore opportunities to improve the overall visual experience of our application using Paper Shaders and Lottie animations.

### 1. Dependencies

Install the following dependencies using Bun inside our frontend Client directory, if they are not already installed:

```bash
bun add @paper-design/shaders-react lottie-react
```

Official resources:
- Paper Shaders: https://shaders.paper.design/
- LottieFiles: https://lottiefiles.com/
- Lottie React: https://lottiereact.com/

Explore their documentation, available effects, animation assets, and implementation examples before making design decisions.

### 2. Paper Shaders

Explore the available shaders, animated textures, organic patterns, ambient backgrounds, and visual effects provided by Paper Shaders.

Study our existing application and identify places where these effects could genuinely enhance the visual experience. Experiment with appropriate textures, subtle background movement, atmospheric effects, and other creative implementations that complement our existing design language.

Don't restrict yourself to obvious background placements. Think creatively about where these effects could add depth, character, and visual sophistication to the application.

### 3. Lottie Animations

Search LottieFiles for high-quality animations that align with our application's identity, purpose, and language-learning experience.

Look for opportunities to introduce meaningful animated illustrations, living visual elements, continuous animation loops, interactive feedback, loading experiences, empty states, and other creative enhancements.

Choose animations that feel naturally integrated into our application rather than externally added decorative assets. Verify their licensing before incorporating them.

### 4. Creative Freedom and Design Consistency

These are exploratory improvements, not mandatory implementation requirements. Do not force Paper Shaders or Lottie animations into components simply because these tools are available.

- Prioritize our existing application theme, color palette, visual identity, and design consistency.
- Think independently about where these tools can genuinely improve the user experience.
- Feel free to explore creative ideas beyond the obvious use cases.
- Make the application feel alive, engaging, polished, and thoughtfully designed.
- Avoid excessive animations, distracting effects, generic gradients, and unnecessary visual clutter.
- Maintain readability, accessibility, responsiveness, and performance.
- Ensure continuous animations are subtle, properly optimized, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Use our existing Motion, Anime.js, GSAP, React Bits, and 21st.dev capabilities wherever they complement these improvements.

### 5. Taste Skill Review

Use Taste Skill throughout the exploration and implementation process, and conduct a final visual review of the actual rendered application.

Critically evaluate whether each enhancement makes our application more beautiful, cohesive, engaging, and enjoyable to use.

If an effect feels unnecessary, generic, distracting, or inconsistent with our application's identity, refine it or remove it entirely.

### Overall Objective

The objective is not to maximize the number of animations or visual effects. It is to elevate the overall experience through thoughtful creative decisions and attention to detail.

Use your own intelligence and design judgment. Experiment wherever appropriate, explore unconventional ideas, and implement only the improvements that genuinely make our application better.

Application theme and design consistency remain the highest priorities. If either library does not provide meaningful improvements, do not force its usage and remove any unused dependencies.

Proceed with exploration, implementation, and visual verification rather than stopping at recommendations.
